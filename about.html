WHO WE ARE

Born From a Love of Bold Flavors

CHOP 46 isn't just a restaurant — it's a flavor movement. Nestled in the heart of Manisha Nagar, Kalwa, we started with a simple mission: to bring authentic, street-style Chinese food with a premium twist to the people of Thane.

🔥 Our Philosophy

We believe great food shouldn't cost a fortune. Every dish at CHOP 46 is crafted with fresh ingredients, bold spices, and the kind of wok-fired passion that you can taste in every bite. From our signature Chicken Crispy to the legendary Cheese King Rice, we pour our heart into every plate.

🍜 The CHOP 46 Experience

Walk in and you'll feel it — the sizzle of the wok, the aroma of garlic and chili, the energy of a kitchen that never stops creating. Whether you're dining in, grabbing a drive-through bite, or ordering from your couch, we promise the same bold, unforgettable experience.

OUR JOURNEY

The CHOP 46 Story

The Beginning

A Dream Takes Shape

It all started with a simple idea — bring bold, street-style Chinese flavors to Kalwa at affordable prices. With a small shop and a big dream, CHOP 46 was born.

The Menu

Crafting the Perfect Menu

Every recipe was tested, tweaked, and perfected. From the Chicken Crispy's secret spice blend to the Dragon Chicken's fiery kick — each dish went through dozens of iterations.

Growing
